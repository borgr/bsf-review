# Broader Impacts Statement

**Drought memory in sediment microbiomes** — Application No. XXXX — A. Levi (Weizmann Institute of Science); B. Jones (University of Michigan)

Rivers in both countries are entering a regime of repeated drought. If sediment communities carry a memory of past droughts, then a river's capacity to recover nutrient cycling after rewetting depends on its history, which current water-quality models ignore. Establishing whether that memory exists, and how long it lasts, would give river managers a measurable indicator of resilience. The project trains two students and a postdoc in field sampling and in quantitative modelling across both sites.
